SABLE YARD

A TACTICAL ROUND-BASED FPS · THREE.JS

Vanguard (you + 2 squad bots) vs Crimson (4 bots). First team to 5 rounds takes the match. Win a round by eliminating the enemy team — or play the objective: Crimson carries the data spike and will try to plant it at Site A (west yard) or Site B (east warehouse). If it plants, hold the site for 35 seconds and it detonates. Defuse it (hold E beside it) or stop the plant to win the round.

Earn money for kills and round wins, then press B during the buy phase to buy rifles, armour and grenades. Stop, crouch and aim — firing on the move sprays wide, and headshots pay double.
